01About

I turn messy, real-world data into systems people can rely on.

I'm a Data Engineer with a degree in Electronics & Communication Engineering from JNTU Hyderabad and an MSc in Computer Science with Advanced Practice from Northumbria University, Newcastle. My work sits where data engineering meets applied AI: building pipelines, modelling data and shipping solutions on AWS, Azure and Databricks.

At Northumbrian Water I developed a mobile app that detects cracks and faults in civil assets using Microsoft Power Apps and Custom Vision. Today, at IIT Hyderabad, I contribute to speech technology for regional Indian dialects under the National Language Translation Mission.

Outside work I'm a regular at tech meetups, first in Newcastle, London and Edinburgh and now in Hyderabad, and I've spoken at BBC Tech Meetup about how AI affects critical thinking and mental health.
